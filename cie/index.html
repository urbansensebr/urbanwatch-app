CIE · Strategic Innovation Consultant

Seeing opportunities before they become obvious.

CIE means Strategic Innovation Consultant.

A professional role dedicated to observing businesses, people and markets through strategic thinking. By connecting ideas, technology, experience and genuine relationships, a CIE helps transform hidden opportunities into practical innovation.

What a CIE does

A Strategic Innovation Consultant does not arrive selling ready-made answers. First, he listens. He observes the context, understands the operation, identifies silent bottlenecks and discovers possibilities that are often hidden inside the daily routine.

Business Vision

Reads the scenario, the people, the market and the hidden potential behind the operation.

Strategic Questions

Helps companies ask better questions before spending money on random solutions.

Simple Innovation

Innovation is not always expensive. Sometimes a small adjustment opens a big portal.

Opportunity Design

Transforms loose ideas into practical paths, services, experiences and revenue models.

Where innovation can appear

Customer Experience

Better journeys, better attention, better trust.

Technology

Portals, NFC cards, digital tools, automation and intelligent interfaces.

Communication

Clear positioning, stronger narrative and more professional presentation.

Revenue Models

New offers, recurring services, partnerships and strategic packages.

The UrbanWatch Method

The UrbanWatch method starts with real conversation. No noise, no generic formula. The goal is to understand the moment, identify what already exists, and connect invisible dots until a new path becomes visible.

Listen

Understand the person, the company and the real pain behind the request.

Observe

Look at processes, customer experience, communication and missed opportunities.

Connect

Bring together ideas, people, tools and possibilities.

Open Portals

Create practical access points to new value, new clients and new strategic moves.

Global Mindset

Innovation has no borders. The CIE concept was born in Brazil with an international mindset, prepared to connect entrepreneurs, professionals and organizations across different cultures and languages.

Every genuine connection is a potential portal to new opportunities.
