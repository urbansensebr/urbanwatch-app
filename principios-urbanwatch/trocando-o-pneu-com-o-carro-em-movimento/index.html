🚗 Changing a Tyre Whilst the Vehicle Is in Motion

Status: Principle of Reality

Priority: High

Origin

This reflection emerged after recognising that there is often an expectation that life must first become organised before meaningful action can begin.

Practical experience has demonstrated precisely the opposite.

Observation

A common illusion persists:

“When I have more time, I shall do it.”
“When I have more money, I shall begin.”
“When I am prepared, I shall move forward.”
“When everything is organised, I shall execute.”

Reality, however, rarely operates in this manner.

Core Insight

Most people do not build their lives after everything is ready.

They build whilst everything is still unfolding.

They learn whilst executing.

They correct whilst advancing.

They discover whilst experimenting.

The Metaphor

For a long time, it seemed impossible to imagine someone changing a tyre whilst the vehicle was still in motion.

The idea appeared absurd, unfeasible, dangerous and irrational.

Until it became clear that, in a certain sense, this is precisely how life occurs.

The Human Reality

No one receives the following before beginning:

  • ✓ a complete manual;
  • ✓ unlimited resources;
  • ✓ absolute guarantees;
  • ✓ a perfect scenario.

On the contrary, most people:

  • 🚗 work whilst learning;
  • 🚗 educate whilst maturing;
  • 🚗 undertake ventures whilst discovering;
  • 🚗 lead whilst still carrying doubts;
  • 🚗 build whilst correcting mistakes.

Principle

Life rarely waits until we are ready.
It unfolds whilst we are preparing.

UrbanWatch Application

UrbanWatch was born under this same condition.

It did not emerge as a finished structure.

It was built whilst:

  • learning;
  • failing;
  • adjusting;
  • testing;
  • observing.

Girassol 9A Application 🌻

Girassol 9A likewise did not begin with a complete methodology.

It was born through movement.

Through experimentation.

Through observation.

Through discovery.

Personal Application

Many of the most significant discoveries emerge during the journey itself.

Not before it.

Necessary Balance

This does not imply imprudence.

It does not mean jumping without checking the safety line.

It does not mean ignoring risks.

It means understanding the difference between:

  • ⚠️ irresponsibility;
  • 🌱 continuous evolution.

Proposed Quotation

I spent a considerable part of my life waiting for the ideal moment to begin.

Then I realised that almost everyone is simply doing the best they can whilst learning.

In the end, we are all changing a tyre whilst the vehicle remains in motion.

Final Record

Perhaps maturity is not the possession of all answers.

Perhaps it is the willingness to continue advancing whilst accepting that some answers will only appear along the road.
